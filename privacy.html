1. Overview

HribSpots is an outdoor foraging spot recorder, GPX track recorder, and interactive map application. We respect your privacy. HribSpots does not require user accounts, does not contain advertisements, and does not sell or rent your personal information.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

Location data (precise and approximate)

  • What is accessed: HribSpots accesses your device’s GPS and network location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) only when you grant location permission.
  • How it is used:
    • To display your current position on the map.
    • To save mushroom foraging waypoints locally on your device.
    • To record GPS trail points in the background when you explicitly start Track Recording (displayed with an active persistent notification until you tap Stop).
  • Local storage: Your saved waypoints, notes, quantities, and GPX tracks are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a backend server and do not upload or collect your saved foraging spots.

Local file and storage access

HribSpots reads and writes GPX waypoint files, track logs, offline map packs/tiles, and app settings (mushmap_settings.json) stored locally on your device so your data remains accessible and portable.

Network requests and third-party map/data services

When your device is connected to the internet and you use online map or lookup features, requests are sent directly from your device to third-party services, which may receive your IP address and the coordinates or search query of the map area you are viewing:

  • Map tiles and elevation data OpenStreetMap, Esri (ArcGIS World Imagery), OpenTopoMap, USGS National Map, EOX Maps, Cooper Hewitt, AWS Terrain Tiles, USFS/Geoplatform services, and Protomaps/Source Cooperative.
  • Place search OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) when you search for a location.
  • Trail routing BRouter (brouter.de) when you generate a route.
  • Species observations iNaturalist API (api.inaturalist.org) when you load nearby fungal observations.

Each third-party provider handles requests in accordance with its own privacy policy. You can use HribSpots offline with locally downloaded maps/regions without making network requests.

3. Data Sharing and Retention

  • No developer servers: We do not collect, store, or have access to your saved spots, notes, tracks, or custom items on any developer-controlled servers.
  • Deletion: You can edit or delete individual waypoints, tracks, and files directly within the app, or remove all app data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling HribSpots from your device.

4. Children’s Privacy

HribSpots is intended for users aged 18 and older (or 16+ depending on local jurisdiction) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Security

All built-in online map and API connections use HTTPS encryption in transit. Locally stored files remain under your control on your device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
